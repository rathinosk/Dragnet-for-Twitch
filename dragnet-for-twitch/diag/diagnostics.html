Checks

What runs on its own while Diagnostics are on. Pick a scenario to set the checks for what is being diagnosed, or switch them one by one (the scenario then shows Custom). Changes apply at once to open Twitch tabs. Probe, Snapshot and the downloads below only run when clicked, so they have no switch.

  • On each drops-page scan: "Claim Now", anything else labelled "claim", a 100% bar without a button, each in-progress campaign block.
  • Keeps checking an open inventory tab after its scan, so a "Claim Now" is saved even if the page is not reloaded by hand. With a reload interval, the tab also reloads itself (each reload is a normal passive scan), in case Twitch does not update progress in place. A label at the bottom left of the Twitch page shows when it is on.
  • Saves the drops responses the campaigns and inventory pages received (needs Twitch page data).
  • On channel pages: anything labelled "claim" other than "Claim Bonus", or matching a guessed claim hook (stream:claim-like).
  • Each distinct set of elements whose hook mentions drops (stream:drop-ui).
  • Toasts and alerts that mention a drop or reward (stream:toast).
  • A channel page that was playing a drops stream and for 5 minutes is not (paused, no player, Drops Enabled tag or game missing), for example after a reload that Twitch did not resume. Saves one capture of the player column per spell (stream:stuck).
  • Saves the drops operations Twitch's player sends, at most once a minute (needs Twitch page data).
  • Shows stall detection's live state on this page (below). Read-only.

Stall detection

What stall detection sees now: each channel tab reporting what it plays, and the drops in progress of its game. A drop counts as stalled after minutes without a rise while the tab plays a drops-enabled stream, seen by at least two inventory reads; the tab is stalled when all of its game's drops are. Updates live and once a minute.

Stall detection is off in Settings: nothing is tracked.
